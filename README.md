A dense one-page newspaper, "THE WORLD TODAY", summing up the world
news of **Wednesday 25 November 2026** in a single 1024x1024 image:
a big date band, an all-text lead story, nine story cards, a THE DAY
IN NUMBERS strip, a six-line IN BRIEF block and an ALSO TODAY ticker
- about 40 of the day's stories on one page.

The day's stories on the page:

1. **Lead - Markets:** Nvidia's rally fades a day after the 31% leap
   (-5.9% Tuesday; Nasdaq -1.5% to 23,734, S&P 500 -0.5%, chip index
   -7.9%); the Dow rises 423 (+0.9%) to 50,019 as money rotates out
   of tech; Anthropic falls below its $83 IPO price.
2. **Ukraine:** Zelenskyy asks for EUR 115bn in foreign aid for 2027;
   Ukraine's war widens beyond Europe - naval drones sank a Russian
   corvette off Libya; a Russian drone strays into Poland.
3. **Middle East:** a Houthi drone hits a Riyadh tower (11 hurt,
   including 2 Indians); Houthis threaten Saudi ports over Hodeidah.
4. **Gaza:** Israel kills a senior Hamas internal-security figure in
   a drone strike; US senators press Israel on winter aid for Gaza.
5. **USA - Cyber:** the CIA and ODNI are hit by a hack affecting
   unclassified information; classified systems are safe.
6. **Trade:** the US readies fresh tariff hikes on China, the EU and
   others to replace levies struck down by the court; investors await
   October PCE inflation and the second Q3 GDP reading.
7. **Climate:** the COP31 collapse fallout - the UN chief voices
   'deep disappointment' as Antalya ends with no deal on fossil
   fuels; talks ran 44 hours past close; next round is COP32.
8. **Asia:** Bangladesh garment workers win a 36% pay rise after
   weeks of strikes; a Hanoi blast kills 6 and hurts 10.
9. **Europe:** the UK records 5,258 heat deaths in summer 2026, the
   highest toll since records began; the EU unveils new Russia
   sanctions on banks, crypto and sport; Norway closes its last coal
   mine, on Svalbard.
10. **Sport:** Real Madrid roar back 5-1 over PSV (Bellingham
    stunner, Mbappe brace) and stay perfect in the Champions League;
    Arsenal 1-1 Dortmund; Man City 2-2 Napoli.

Plus a THE DAY IN NUMBERS strip (Dow, Nasdaq, Brent, Bitcoin, the
EUR 115bn aid ask, 11 hurt in Riyadh, 5,258 UK heat deaths), a
six-line IN BRIEF block (Iranian rial record low, Russian drone in
Poland, Norway's last coal mine, Iraq seizing Kurdish oilfields,
China Southern's 50 Boeing 737-8 order, OPEC+ meeting 29 Nov) and
an ALSO TODAY ticker (Cartagena supermarket hostages, Senegal's
AFCON title restored, Colombia car bomb, Laos leaving
least-developed status).

The image is drawn by `draw_image.py` (Pillow, deterministic) and
committed as `entry.png`; `main.py` copies it to the output directory.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
